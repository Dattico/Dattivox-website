The First Journey

Getting this right is what makes everything else work.

  • A Dattico business lead works alongside you to map how your calls actually flow today — who answers what, what gets missed, and what a good outcome sounds like.
  • We configure Dattivox around that, connect it to your phone system, and stay with you through the first live calls until it answers the way you would.
  • Included on Business, Scale and Enterprise on a 12-month term. On Starter and Pro it is a fixed one-off, so you pay only for the time it takes.
  • Need deeper accompaniment than your package covers? Extra days can be added to any plan.

Not just voice minutes

Most competitors sell AI minutes. Dattivox delivers a call operation: every call lands in your CRM, gets analysed, and proposes a follow-up. That back-office work is what you're not hiring for.

The First Journey matters

A business person maps how your calls actually flow and configures Dattivox around that. Self-serve platforms leave this to you and your team, which is where most AI voice projects stall.

EU compliance is not optional

Dattivox, Famulor and Elora are EU-hosted and GDPR-ready by default. US platforms require extra configuration or enterprise contracts for EU data residency and the AI Act.

Scale pricing reflects scale service

At Scale and Enterprise, Dattivox includes a dedicated change consultant, custom API integrations, model training on your call history, SSO and a 99,9% uptime SLA with credits. Most competitors don't publish an SLA at all.
